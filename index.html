Call Center Intelligence

Turns call-center audio into transcripts, QA scorecards, and compliance reports. A seven-node LangGraph pipeline checks for prompt injection and redacts personal data before any model call, scores calls with a deterministic engine that overrides raw model scores, and sends results to a supervisor for review. 143 tests; runs on Hugging Face Spaces.
